Government Agricultural Schemes for Farmers — Northeast India / Assam

PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
Direct income support of Rs. 6,000 per year to farmer families owning cultivable land.
Payment: Three equal installments of Rs. 2,000 every four months (April-July, August-November, December-March)
Eligibility: All landholding farmer families (small, marginal, large) with cultivable land
Registration: Through local Patwari/Revenue Officer, Common Service Centre (CSC), or PM-KISAN portal
Documents needed: Aadhaar card, land records (Jamabandi/Khatiyan), bank account (linked to Aadhaar)
Assam contact: Directorate of Agriculture, Assam — 0361-2237870
Not eligible: Institutional land holders, government employees, pensioners above Rs. 10,000/month

PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana) — Crop Insurance
Insurance coverage for crop losses due to natural calamities, pests, diseases.
Premium rates: Kharif crops 2% of sum insured; Rabi crops 1.5%; Horticulture/commercial crops 5%
Claim settlement: Based on Crop Cutting Experiments (CCE) data
Coverage in Assam: Paddy, maize, potato, mustard, brinjal, tomato, ginger
Enrollment: Through bank where crop loan is taken, or at CSC, or insurance company office
Enrollment deadline: Within 10 days before crop sowing for Kharif; varies for Rabi
Important for Assam: Covers flood damage — very relevant given Brahmaputra flooding

PM Kisan Maandhan (PM-KMY) — Pension Scheme
Monthly pension of Rs. 3,000 per month after age 60.
Eligibility: Small and marginal farmers aged 18–40 years; land up to 2 hectares
Contribution: Rs. 55 to Rs. 200 per month depending on enrollment age (government matches)
Registration: At CSC or through PM-Kisan portal

Agricultural Infrastructure Fund (AIF)
Financial support for post-harvest infrastructure and community farming assets.
Coverage: Cold storage, grading/sorting units, primary processing units, warehouses
Loan amount: Up to Rs. 2 crore; 3% interest subvention
Eligible: Farmers, FPOs, PACS, marketing cooperative societies
Application: Through bank or NABARD-empanelled financial institutions

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
Short-term credit for crop cultivation, post-harvest expenses, maintenance of farm assets.
Credit limit: Based on land holding and cropping pattern
Interest: 4% per annum for prompt repayment (government subvention on 7% interest)
Coverage: Now extended to animal husbandry and fisheries farmers
Apply: Any nationalized bank, cooperative bank, or regional rural bank

ATMA Scheme (Support to State Extension Programme — SSEP)
Farm demonstrations, farmer field schools, exposure visits
Contact: Block Technology Team (BTT) at local block agriculture office
Provides: Free or subsidized demonstrations of new crop varieties and technologies

National Horticulture Mission (NHM) / MIDH
Subsidies for:
- Establishment of orchards (50% subsidy on planting material cost)
- Protected cultivation: Polyhouse/net house (50–75% subsidy)
- Drip and sprinkler irrigation (55% subsidy for small farmers)
- Post-harvest infrastructure
Apply: Through District Horticulture Officer (DHO), Assam

PM Krishi Sinchai Yojana — Har Khet Ko Pani, More Crop Per Drop
Drip irrigation subsidy: 55% for small/marginal farmers; 45% for others
Sprinkler subsidy: Similar structure
Application: Through Agriculture/Horticulture Department of Assam

Soil Health Card Scheme
Free soil testing once every 2 years
Soil Health Card provides: NPK status, micronutrient deficiencies, recommendations
Contact: Nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or State Soil Testing Laboratory
Assam: State Soil Testing Laboratory, Jorhat, Assam — 0376-2322073

Assam State-Specific Schemes

Mukhyamantri Samagra Gramya Unnayan Yojana (MGVUY)
Assam state scheme for integrated rural development
Provides: Seeds, fertilizers, farm equipment at subsidized rates to small farmers

Chief Minister's Samagra Gramya Unnayan Yojana
Assam scheme: Vegetable cultivation support for small farmers
Includes: Seed kits, training, marketing linkage support

AGROMET Services — IMD Assam
Free agro-weather advisories issued twice weekly by Guwahati Meteorological Centre
Advisories cover: Irrigation, pest management, harvesting recommendations based on weather forecasts
Access: Through Kisan Portal, Meghdoot app, or local Krishi Vigyan Kendra

Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) — Extension Services
KVKs are the primary on-ground extension agency in every district of Assam
Services: Soil testing, demonstration plots, farmer training, seed distribution
For Brinjal/Vegetable growers: Contact nearest KVK for:
  - New variety demonstration
  - Pest management advisory
  - Organic farming conversion support
KVK Kamrup: Assam Agricultural University Campus, Guwahati — 0361-2347222
KVK Nagaon: Nagaon — 03672-233144

How to Access Subsidies
1. Contact your local Block Agriculture Office (BAO)
2. Register on DBT Agriculture portal (farmer.gov.in)
3. Link Aadhaar with bank account for direct benefit transfer
4. Keep land documents updated (Jamabandi, patta)
5. Form or join a Farmer Producer Organization (FPO) for better scheme access
