Tomato Cultivation Guide — Northeast India (ICAR)

Climate and Soil Requirements
Tomato grows best at temperatures between 20°C and 27°C. Night temperatures below 10°C cause flower drop and fruit setting failure. In Northeast India, tomato is grown from October to March in plains and from March to June in hills. Well-drained loamy soil with pH 6.0 to 7.0 is ideal. Avoid waterlogged fields.

Variety Selection
Recommended varieties for Northeast India: Pusa Ruby, Pusa Sheetal, Arka Vikas, Himsona. Hybrid varieties such as Avinash-2 and Naveen give higher yield but require more inputs. Use disease-resistant varieties like Arka Rakshak for areas prone to late blight.

Land Preparation and Transplanting
Plough field 3–4 times to fine tilth. Apply 25 tonnes of well-rotted farmyard manure per hectare before final ploughing. Transplant seedlings at 4–5 leaf stage, spacing 60×45 cm. Transplant in evening to reduce transplanting shock.

Fertilizer Application
Apply NPK at 120:60:60 kg per hectare. Apply full P, K, and one-third N as basal dose. Apply remaining N in two equal splits at 30 and 60 days after transplanting. Foliar spray of 0.5% boron at flowering increases fruit set.

Irrigation
Tomato requires 600–1200 mm water per season. Irrigate immediately after transplanting. Provide irrigation at 7–10 day intervals. Avoid water stress during flowering and fruit development. Drip irrigation increases water use efficiency by 40–50%.

Staking and Training
Stake plants at 30 cm height using bamboo or wooden stakes. Single stem training gives higher early yield. Remove suckers weekly for indeterminate varieties. Train plants using plastic twine for better air circulation and disease management.

Harvesting
First harvest 60–70 days after transplanting. Harvest at mature green or breaker stage for long-distance transport. For local markets, harvest at pink to light red stage. Average yield: 25–35 tonnes per hectare under good management.

Post-Harvest
Sort fruits removing damaged and diseased ones. Grade by size: Grade A (above 50g), Grade B (30–50g), Grade C (below 30g). Store at 10–12°C and 90% relative humidity for 2–3 weeks. Use ventilated crates for transport.
