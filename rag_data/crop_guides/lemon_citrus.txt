Lemon and Citrus Cultivation Guide — Northeast India / Assam

Overview
Lemon (Citrus limon) and Acid Lime (Citrus aurantifolia) are important fruit crops in Northeast India. Assam is known for producing the Assam Lemon (Citrus limon var. assamensis) — a large, round, thick-skinned variety highly valued locally. Khasi mandarin orange (Citrus reticulata) is also extensively grown in Meghalaya and hilly parts of Assam.

Climate and Soil
- Optimum temperature: 25°C to 30°C
- Annual rainfall: 1500–2000 mm (Assam has ideal rainfall for citrus)
- Soil: well-drained deep loamy soil, pH 5.5–6.5
- Avoid waterlogged soils — citrus is very sensitive to root rot in waterlogged conditions
- Assam's humid subtropical climate is well-suited for Assam lemon cultivation

Planting Season in Assam
- Best planting time: February to March (spring) or August to September (monsoon end)
- Avoid planting during peak monsoon (June–July) — risk of root rot
- Grafted plants give better results than seedlings; buy from certified nurseries

Varieties Suitable for Assam
1. Assam Lemon (Citrus limon var. assamensis) — large round fruits, thin rind, highly aromatic, adapted to Assam conditions
2. Kagzi Lime (Citrus aurantifolia) — small round fruits, very juicy, high acid content, good for pickles
3. Eureka Lemon — good for plains, high yield
4. Lisbon Lemon — thornless, suitable for Brahmaputra valley
5. Khasi Mandarin — for hilly areas, very sweet, October–December harvest

Planting
- Dig pits 60×60×60 cm
- Fill with mixture: top soil + FYM (10 kg) + SSP 500g + neem cake 1 kg
- Plant grafted saplings at pit center
- Spacing: 4×4 m to 6×6 m depending on variety
- Stake young plants against Assam's strong monsoon winds

Fertilizer Schedule (per tree per year, mature tree 5+ years)
- FYM: 50 kg (apply in February and August)
- N: 500g (Urea 1.1 kg) — split February, June, October
- P₂O₅: 250g (SSP 1.6 kg) — apply February
- K₂O: 250g (MOP 400g) — split February and June
- Young trees: use half the above dose in Year 1, increase gradually

Micronutrient Management
Assam soils are prone to zinc, iron, and magnesium deficiency in citrus:
- Zinc: foliar spray ZnSO₄ 0.5% (May and September)
- Iron chlorosis: spray FeSO₄ 0.5% + Citric acid 0.1%
- Magnesium: MgSO₄ 0.5% foliar spray at flowering

Irrigation
- Critical: flowering (February–March) and fruit development (June–September)
- Young orchards: irrigate every 7–10 days in dry season
- Mature trees: irrigate every 15 days; reduce after fruit colouring
- Mulching with rice straw conserves moisture, common practice in Assam

Training and Pruning
- Allow 3–4 main scaffold branches
- Annual light pruning after harvest: remove dead, crossing, and water shoots
- Do not over-prune — lemon bears on 1–2 year old wood

Major Pests and Diseases in Assam
Citrus Canker (Xanthomonas axonopodis):
- Very common in Assam's humid climate
- Symptoms: raised corky lesions on fruit, leaves, twigs
- Control: Copper oxychloride 3g/liter spray every 15 days during monsoon; remove infected branches

Citrus Psylla (Diaphorina citri):
- Transmits Citrus Greening (Huanglongbing) — most destructive citrus disease
- Control: Imidacloprid 0.3 ml/liter; remove and burn infected trees

Leaf Miner (Phyllocnistis citrella):
- Attacks new flush (young leaves)
- Control: Spinosad 0.5 ml/liter at new flush emergence

Sooty Mould:
- Secondary to scale insects and mealybugs
- Control: Dimethoate 2 ml/liter followed by starch paste wash

Fruit Drop Management
- Pre-harvest fruit drop is a major problem in Assam lemon
- Spray 2,4-D at 10 ppm (1 ml in 100 liters) to reduce fruit drop
- Spray at marble stage fruit development (April–May)

Harvest and Yield
- Bearing starts in 3–4 years from grafted plants (7–10 years from seeds — avoid seeds)
- Harvest when fruits attain full size and turn yellowish-green
- Acid lime: harvest slightly immature for more juice content
- Yield: 40–60 fruits per tree initially, 100–150 fruits in full bearing
- Mature Assam lemon orchard: 4–6 tonnes per hectare
- Harvest season: October to January main crop; minor crop May–June

Marketing
- Best prices in Guwahati: Fancy Bazar market
- Assam lemon fetches premium in metros due to aromatic quality
- Value addition: lemon pickle, lemon squash, lemon essential oil
- APEDA supports export of Assam citrus to Middle East and Southeast Asia
